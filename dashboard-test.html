Reading the sheet. Figures blend the 2025/26 full season with this season so far — the new-season weight climbs to 50% by gameweek 10. Goals come from an attack-versus-defence strength model with a home tilt; corners sum each side's 2025/26 corners-taken rate; cards sum each side's yellow-card rate. Ticks on each meter mark the common betting lines. The juice score (0–100) weights goals 40, cards 35, corners 25.

Last weekend. The results band scores each suggested line against what actually happened (goals from the final score; corners and cards from ESPN's match stats, which can lag a few hours). The model line is recomputed from current data, so it's a close guide to — not an exact replay of — what the sheet showed before kickoff.

Not modelled: the referee (the biggest card swing), team news, form momentum, weather, or the odds themselves. Check live prices in your bookmaker's app and only back legs you rate yourself.
