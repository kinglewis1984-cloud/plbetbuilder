What this is. A free weekly prediction game built on a statistical model with known blind spots. It's for entertainment — nothing here is financial advice.

Reading the sheet. Figures blend the 2025/26 full season with this season so far — the new-season weight climbs to 50% by gameweek 10. Goals come from an attack-versus-defence strength model with a home tilt; corners sum each side's 2025/26 corners-taken rate; cards sum each side's yellow-card rate. Ticks on each meter mark the scoring bands. The juice score (0–100) weights goals 40, cards 35, corners 25.

Last weekend. The band takes the model's prediction for each metric and checks whether it landed against what actually happened (goals from the final score; corners and cards from ESPN's match stats, which can lag a few hours). Predictions are recomputed from current data, so it's a close guide to — not an exact replay of — what the sheet showed before kickoff.

Not modelled: the referee (the biggest card swing), team news, form momentum, and weather.
